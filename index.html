01 / About

Research with purpose.
Teaching with impact.

Dr. Raavi Deepthi is an Associate Professor of Computer Science and Engineering with 11 years of academic experience in higher education, research and academic publishing. Her work spans cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning and intelligent intrusion detection.

Her research focuses on secure and privacy-conscious systems for the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), including federated learning, feature selection, class-imbalance handling, anomaly detection and deep learning architectures. Additional interests include digital twins, smart grids, healthcare AI, computer vision, autonomous systems and IoT.

Her academic contributions include teaching, research, scholarly publishing, book and chapter development, editorial service, peer review, invited speaking engagements, patent activity and externally funded research.
